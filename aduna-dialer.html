FAQ

Questions, answered

Do I need my own phone numbers?
No. Aduna provisions local-presence numbers for each rep automatically, so every dial goes out with a familiar local area code. Bring-your-own numbers are also supported if you prefer.
Does it integrate with my calendar?
Yes. One-click meeting booking drops the demo straight into your connected calendar, pre-filled with the prospect's details — booked live while you're still on the call.
How many people can use it?
As many as you like — add a seat for each rep at $100 per month. Admins manage the whole floor while agents see only their own leads, so the same setup works for one closer or a hundred.
What do the calls themselves cost?
Talk time, phone numbers, and text messages are billed at the carrier's cost and passed straight through — we add no markup. As a guide, a rep dialing US numbers full-time typically runs about $1–2 per hour of active dialing, and every call is itemized in your dashboard in real time. You are never charged for minutes you didn't use.
Can two reps share one seat?
Each seat is licensed to one person. Sign-ins are single-session, so if the same login is used somewhere else the earlier session is signed out automatically. That keeps shared passwords out of your organization and makes sure every call, booking, and stat is credited to the rep who earned it.
Can I upload my own lead lists?
Yes — drop in any CSV and it becomes a ready-to-dial call list. Columns map automatically (you can adjust them), numbers already in your CRM are skipped so nobody gets double-called, and you can assign the list to a rep as you import it.
Is my data secure?
Every call, lead, and note is stored in a secure Postgres database with access scoped to your team and role-based permissions, so reps only ever see the data they're meant to.
